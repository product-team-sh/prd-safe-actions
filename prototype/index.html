Safe actions prototype

A clickable prototype of how Saleshandy protects risky actions: the selection says its real scope, risky buttons carry words and sit apart, every risky action opens a dialog that says what it affects, and bulk changes can be undone for 10 seconds. Each screen is built on production markup and the production stylesheet, so what you see is what ships; anything new is outlined when you switch on "What's new". The spec is the source of truth for copy and states.

How to use the demo bar

  1. The dark bar at the top is not part of the product. Its controls change the scenario: how big the selection is, whether the breakdown loads, is slow or fails, and whether the action succeeds or fails.
  2. "Today" shows production as it is now, so you can compare before and after on the same data. "Proposed" is the change.
  3. "What's new" outlines, with a purple dashed line, every element production does not have yet.
  4. "Dev notes" opens a panel with each spec row on the page: the data the dialog needs, whether undo applies, and links to today's code at the pinned commit.
  5. Esc or a click outside a dialog cancels it, exactly as it should in the product.
